About Us

Who We Are

Guided by our motto — “General Solution to Engineering and IT/ICT Consultancy” — we provide practical, high-quality engineering and technology solutions.

Gobby Contractors & Consultants Ltd.

Gobby Contractors & Consultants Ltd. (formerly Gobby Communication Ltd.) is an indigenous Nigerian engineering, construction, and IT/ICT consultancy firm headquartered at No 10, Oba Adegboye Plaza, Mogadishu Layout, Kaduna, Kaduna North Local Government.

Our team of experienced engineers, builders, quantity surveyors, ICT specialists, and environmental health professionals works closely with public institutions, corporate organizations, and communities to deliver durable infrastructure and reliable technology solutions.

Our Mission

To deliver civil, building, electrical, water, and IT/ICT projects with high quality, safety, and timely execution.

Our Vision

To be a trusted partner of choice for engineering construction and ICT consultancy across Nigeria.

Our Services

Core Areas of Expertise

Comprehensive solutions across engineering, construction, renewable energy, and information technology.

Civil & Building Construction

Construction of institutional buildings, museums, reception halls, perimeter fencing, drainage systems, and general civil engineering works.

School & Clinic Renovation

Complete rehabilitation of primary and secondary schools, healthcare clinics, administrative offices, roofing, terrazzo flooring, and furniture supply.

Electrical & Solar Power Systems

Electrical contracting works, solar photovoltaic (PV) installations, inverter and battery bank setups, and street lighting projects.

IT / ICT Consultancy

Supply and installation of computers, printers, networking equipment, enterprise software deployment, and technical ICT advisory.

Borehole & Water Works

Community and institutional borehole drilling, submersible pump installation, overhead steel water tanks, and water distribution systems.

Media & General Contracting

Public enlightenment broadcast consultancy, multilingual media production, and general procurement and supply services.
